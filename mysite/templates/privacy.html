1. Log Files and Browsing Data

Like most standard websites, A.A.News utilizes log files automatically. The general data collected through these log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses.
  • Browser type (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari).
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP).

This information is completely anonymous and is not linked to any data that can identify you personally. The primary purpose of collecting this data is to manage the website, analyze trends, and improve the overall user experience.

Google Analytics uses its own cookies to analyze web traffic. For more detailed information on how Google uses data, you can review the Google Privacy & Terms page.

We use cookies to enhance and streamline our readers' experience. Cookies are small data files saved on your device through your web browser. They help us remember your preferences and ensure the site loads faster for subsequent visits. You can choose to disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

  • DART cookies enable Google to serve relevant ads based on a visitor's previous internet browsing history (including visits to our site and other sites on the internet).
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy page.

Once you click on these links and leave our site, their respective privacy policies will apply. If a parent or guardian believes that our database contains the information of a child under the age of 13, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to remove such information.
